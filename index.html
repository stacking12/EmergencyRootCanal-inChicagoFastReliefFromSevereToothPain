Emergency Root Canal Treatment in Chicago, IL

A root canal has a reputation it doesn’t deserve. Today, an emergency root canal is a comfortable, routine procedure that eliminates the infection causing your pain. Waiting usually makes things worse: the infection can spread to the jaw and surrounding tissue, and the tooth may no longer be savable.

If you notice any of these warning signs, call us today:

Severe or throbbing toothache
Lingering hot/cold sensitivity
Swollen gums or face
Pimple-like bump on the gum
Pain when biting or chewing
Darkened or discolored tooth

FAQs

Emergency Root Canal & Urgent Dental Care FAQs

Quick answers to the questions we hear most from patients in pain.

When do I need an emergency root canal?
You may need an emergency root canal if you have severe or throbbing tooth pain, lingering sensitivity to hot or cold, swelling in the gums or face, a pimple-like bump on the gum, or a tooth that has darkened. These can be signs of an infected or inflamed nerve that needs prompt care.
Is an emergency root canal painful?
The procedure is done under local anesthesia, so most patients feel pressure rather than pain. The treatment removes the source of your pain, and many people feel relief soon after the infected tissue is cleaned out.
Can I get a same day emergency root canal appointment?
We work to see urgent patients as quickly as possible, often the same day. Call 773-373-0971 and describe your symptoms so our team can schedule you at the earliest opening.
What should I do while waiting for my emergency dental visit?
Rinse gently with warm water, apply a cold compress to the outside of your cheek, avoid very hot or cold foods, and take over-the-counter pain medication as directed on the label. Do not place aspirin directly on the gum. If you have facial swelling or trouble breathing or swallowing, go to the nearest emergency room right away.
Do you offer affordable emergency dental care?
Yes. We aim to provide professional emergency dental care at fair, transparent prices. Call us to discuss your treatment options, payment questions and insurance before your visit.
